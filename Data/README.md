## **Dataset Description**

The dataset contains detailed **sales transaction records** with both order-level and product-level information.  
It covers the entire sales process including order dates, customer details, territories, product categories, and financial metrics.

---

### **Key Fields Include:**

- **SalesOrderID, SalesOrderDetailID** – Unique identifiers for each order and its line items.  
- **OrderDate, DueDate, ShipDate** – Dates related to order placement, due date, and shipping.  
- **CustomerID, TerritoryID, Territory** – Customer reference and geographic sales territory.  
- **SubTotal, TaxAmt, Freight, TotalDue** – Financial details including product subtotal, taxes, shipping cost, and final amount due.  
- **OrderQty** – Quantity of the product ordered.  
- **ProductID, Product** – Product identifier and product name.  
- **ProductCategoryID, Category** – Main product category (e.g., Bikes, Clothing, Accessories, Components).  
- **ProductSubcategoryID, Subcategory** – More specific grouping within categories (e.g., Road Bikes, Mountain Bikes, Bottles and Cages).  
- **Color** – Product color.

---

This dataset provides a **comprehensive overview of sales activity**, enabling analysis across **time, geography, product attributes, and revenue performance**.
